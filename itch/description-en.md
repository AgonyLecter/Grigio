# GRIGIO
### *Every day is Monday.*

**Perpetua Assicurazioni S.p.A. — Claims Department — 4th floor — Desk 4B-17.**

The alarm goes off at 06:47. Tram 14. Badge. Claims. Coffee that tastes like burnt paper. Stamp, stamp, stamp. Home. Sleep.

The alarm goes off at 06:47.

Every day is Monday, and every Monday the world gets a little grayer. The clock stops moving. The windows turn out to be photographs. Your coworkers' faces start to fade — and so does yours, in the bathroom mirror.

But the gray has cracks. A thin orange line behind the buildings at dawn. A tram stop you've never gotten off at. A dying plant that only needs a glass of water. A question nobody has asked in years: *"Are you happy?"*

Find the **seven fragments of color**. Find the key. Find the red door at the end of the hall.

Or stay seated. Everyone else does.

---

**GRIGIO** (Italian for *gray*) is a short psychological horror game about routine, apathy and depression — and about the small cracks where the light gets in.

### Features
- A hand-drawn, fully procedural world that loses its color the more you surrender to the routine — and gets it back, one fragment at a time
- A paperwork minigame where approving the wrong claim might be the most important thing you ever do
- People worth talking to: Marta, who used to paint; Gianni, who used to sing; Osvaldo at the front desk; your sister Chiara, who never stops calling
- Several endings — get out alone, get out together, or be absorbed by the Process
- **Shared Mondays:** leave a note on the office fridge for other players to read, see how many employees are on shift right now
- Dynamic sound design: office hum, horror drone, and a melody that grows with every color you find
- Fully playable in **English and Italian**
- Plays in the browser (desktop and mobile, landscape) or as a Windows app

### Controls
- **Mouse / touch** — interact, advance the dialogue
- **Space / Enter** — advance the dialogue · **1–4** — pick a choice
- **≡** button — menu, audio, language
- Best played with **headphones**, in the dark

### Content warning
This game deals with depression, emotional numbness and alienation at work. It contains unsettling imagery and mild jump scares. There is no gore.
If you recognize yourself in this story, please talk to someone: a friend, a family member, your doctor, a therapist. You are not a claim to be rejected.
